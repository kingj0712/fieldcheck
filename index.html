This world

Plankton blooms on its own. Fish school together, graze on plankton, and spend energy as they swim — eat enough and they spawn young; starve and they fade. Each fish carries small heritable traits (speed, size, sense, color), so over time the pool evolves: lean times favor the small and efficient, hunters favor the quick.

Hunters chase the nearest fish they can sense. They starve without a catch, and a fallen hunter returns to the pool as plankton. When the pool empties, the tide quietly restocks it — no ending, just cycles.

Tools

1 Feed — sprinkle plankton where you drag.
2 Stir — push the water; everything nearby scatters.
3 Fish — release a fish at the cursor.
4 Hunter — release a hunter. Use sparingly.
